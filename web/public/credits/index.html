Data and credits

We acknowledge the use of data and/or imagery from NASA's Fire Information for Resource Management System (FIRMS) (https://earthdata.nasa.gov/firms), part of NASA's Land, Atmosphere Near real-time Capability for Earth observations (LANCE) (https://earthdata.nasa.gov/lance) and NASA's Earth Science Data and Information System (ESDIS).

Data used

  • MODIS Collection 6.1 active fire (Terra and Aqua), standard processing, FIRMS yearly country files and FIRMS API.
  • VIIRS 375 m active fire (S-NPP and NOAA-20), standard processing, FIRMS yearly country files and FIRMS API.
  • VIIRS NOAA-20 near-real-time active fire, FIRMS API.

References

  • Schroeder, W., Oliva, P., Giglio, L., & Csiszar, I. A. (2014). The New VIIRS 375 m active fire detection data product: Algorithm description and initial assessment. Remote Sensing of Environment, 143, 85–96.
  • Davies, D. K., Ilavajhala, S., Wong, M. M., & Justice, C. O. (2009). Fire Information for Resource Management System: Archiving and distributing MODIS active fire data. IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing, 47(1), 72–79.
  • NASA Earthdata (2026). Transitioning from Suomi NPP VIIRS & OMPS: What FIRMS Users Need to Know.
