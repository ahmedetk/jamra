Alerts

Is this month unusual here? Checked every 3 hours against the same days in past years.

Live counts come from VIIRS NOAA-20 (S-NPP stopped on 2 Nov 2026) and are converted to the S-NPP scale of our history: NOAA-20 × 0.97, a factor measured on 2018–2025 data.

Public feeds: alerts.json · RSS · status.json

Not an emergency service: satellite data arrive with hours of delay. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (for example 112 in the EU and Türkiye).
