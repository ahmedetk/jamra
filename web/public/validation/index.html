Validation

How we tested

We built the model on development years and kept the last four years hidden as a final test. Every number below comes only from those held-out test years.

How it works

  1. 1

    Overlap years

    From 2012 both sensors watched the same fires: MODIS on Aqua and VIIRS on S-NPP pass at the same time of day.

  2. 2

    Conversion factor

    For each country and calendar month we measure VIIRS hotspots per MODIS hotspot; small areas lean on the country value: k = (V + λ·kcountry) / (M + λ).

  3. 3

    Old record on the new scale

    MODIS months before 2012 become VIIRS-equivalent hotspots with an error range, then each month is compared with its usual.

Why 2003? We use MODIS on Aqua only: its first full year is 2003 and it crosses at the same afternoon time as VIIRS. Live data come from NOAA-20 since S-NPP stopped (2 Nov 2026), × 0.97.

Limits

  • Hotspots are not fires and not burned area; clouds and smoke hide fires.
  • Values before 2012 are estimates from MODIS, shown with an error range (wider for small areas).
  • Not every hotspot is a wildfire: farm burning, gas flares, industry and, in conflict areas, explosions.
  • Harmonized history covers Portugal, Spain, Greece, Türkiye, Cyprus, Lebanon, Jordan and Palestine; elsewhere, live data only.
  • Latency is hours. This is not an emergency service.
