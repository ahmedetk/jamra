How it works

  1. 1

    Overlap years

    From 2012 both sensors watched the same fires. MODIS on Aqua and VIIRS on S-NPP pass at the same time of day (about 1:30 pm and 1:30 am).

  2. 2

    Conversion factor

    For each country and calendar month we measure VIIRS hotspots per MODIS hotspot. A small area gets its own factor, pulled toward the country value when it has few detections: k = (V + λ·kcountry) / (M + λ).

  3. 3

    Old record on the new scale

    MODIS months before 2012 become VIIRS-equivalent hotspots with an error range. Each month is then compared with the same month in other years.

Methods compared

Data flow and the 2003 start

History (2003 → last month) comes from NASA FIRMS yearly archive files and the FIRMS API, rebuilt automatically every month. The newest days come from the FIRMS API every 3 hours. Why not fetch 20 years on every click?

The record starts in 2003 because we use MODIS on Aqua only: Aqua's first full year is 2003, and it crosses at the same afternoon time as VIIRS, so the two sensors see the same fires at the same hour. Adding Terra (from 2000/2001) is a next step.

S-NPP stopped on 2 Nov 2026. Live data come from NOAA-20 (same VIIRS instrument), converted to the S-NPP scale with a measured factor (×0.97).

Limits

  • Hotspots are not fires and not burned area. One big fire makes many hotspots.
  • Clouds and smoke hide fires. Small or short fires are missed, MODIS more than VIIRS.
  • Values before 2012 are estimates from MODIS on the VIIRS scale, shown with an error range. Small areas have wider ranges.
  • Outages and orbit drift. Old satellites drift in orbit; the VIIRS/MODIS ratio is checked year by year.
  • S-NPP stopped on 2 Nov 2026. Live data come from NOAA-20 (same VIIRS instrument), converted to the S-NPP scale with a measured factor.
  • Near-real-time and standard data differ slightly. Recent months use near-real-time data until NASA's standard data replace them.
  • Not every hotspot is a wildfire. Farm burning, gas flares, industry and, in conflict areas, explosions or burning buildings can create hotspots. The static-source filter can make mistakes.
  • Coverage: Portugal, Spain, Greece, Türkiye, Cyprus, Lebanon, Jordan and Palestine (outlined on the map).
  • Latency is hours. This is not an emergency service.
